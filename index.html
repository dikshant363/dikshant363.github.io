01 / About

Engineer by building.

I learn by taking projects from architecture and implementation through testing, documentation, and release.

My interests sit at the intersection of applied AI, software engineering, and thoughtful product design. I enjoy working close to the underlying system — integrating native platform capabilities, reusing performant engines, and designing robust pipelines instead of treating AI as a thin API wrapper.

Recent work includes a native macOS anime dubbing workstation, an Android PDF application powered by a shared Rust engine, and a public-interest election research platform. I’m especially interested in local-first AI, agentic software, developer tools, and reliable product systems.

I value measurable engineering, honest documentation, privacy-aware defaults, and software that remains maintainable after the demo.
